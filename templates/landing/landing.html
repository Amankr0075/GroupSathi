Our Objectives

We are on a mission to digitize, secure, and uplift cooperative financial ecosystems across rural and urban landscapes.

Bridging the gap between unbanked communities and structured financial ecosystems.

Total Transparency

The GroupSathi Ecosystem

A seamlessly integrated platform designed for every stakeholder in the Self Help Group network.

For Members

Customers get access to a streamlined mobile and web app. They can join groups, request loans, track EMIs automatically, chat with group members, and get 24/7 AI support.

  • Real-time EMI Tracking
  • Live Group Chat
  • Ticket Support System

For Super Admins

Total control over the platform. Monitor all users, loans, and groups across the network. Intervene when necessary, send broadcast alerts, and manage support escalations.

  • Global Data Oversight
  • One-Click Broadcasts
  • Escalated Ticket Queue

For Tech Staff

Dedicated support portals allow your technical staff to rapidly look up customer data, provide assistance, and escalate critical issues directly to the admin.

  • Fast Customer Lookup
  • Live Support Chat
  • PDF Report Generation

24/7 AI Assistance

Never feel lost in your finances. Our built-in AI Assistant is ready to answer questions, guide you through app features, and help you make informed decisions.

  • Automated EMI Calculations
  • Smart Loan Approvals
  • Instant Report Generation
