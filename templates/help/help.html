GroupSathi enforces a secure, structured onboarding to protect every member's financial identity.

  1. Register your account using your full legal name, mobile number, and a strong password.
  2. Set a 5-Digit Security PIN — a fast, numeric credential for quick logins on mobile and shared devices.
  3. Complete your profile by filling in Gender, full Address, and Pin Code. The system then auto-generates a unique Member ID (e.g. MB102) — your permanent identity across all groups.
PIN-Based Login Auto Member ID Mobile-First
Security Tip: Never share your 5-digit PIN. PIN resets require verification of your registered full name and mobile number on the Forgot PIN page.

Creating a Self-Help Group (SHG) initializes a locked financial repository with custom rules that govern how the group operates.

Configuration Fields (set once at creation):

Monthly EMI Amount
The fixed monthly saving each member contributes (e.g. ₹500). Fixed permanently after creation.
EMI Due Day
The calendar date (1st–28th) of every month when EMI collections are due. Payments are locked to this date only.
Interest Rate
Monthly percentage charged on all loans disbursed from the group pool (e.g. 2% per month).
Late Fine Amount
Penalty automatically applied if a member misses their monthly EMI on the due date.

Leadership Hierarchy:

  • Group Leader: The creator. Holds absolute administrative control — approves all requests, promotes/demotes members, edits group info, and can permanently destroy the group.
  • Co-Leader: Appointed by the Leader. Can approve/reject join, leave, EMI, and loan requests. Can also send manual EMI alerts.
  • Member: Standard participant. Can pay EMI, request loans, request fine waivers, and vote on group consensus decisions.
The group creator automatically becomes the Leader. Promote a trusted member to Co-Leader from the group's Members section to share administrative responsibilities.

All membership changes are structured workflows requiring leader verification to maintain group accountability.

Joining a Group:

  1. Go to Join Group on your dashboard.
  2. Enter the group's unique 6-digit Group ID shared by the leader.
  3. Your request appears in the leader's dashboard under Pending Requests.
  4. The Leader or Co-Leader approves/rejects. On approval, you're instantly activated as a member.

Leaving a Group:

  1. Ensure you have zero outstanding loans and no unpaid EMIs or fines.
  2. Click Leave from the group details page.
  3. The Leader or Co-Leader reviews your leave request and approves it.
Leave requests with any outstanding financial liabilities (loans, fines, EMIs) will be blocked by the system and cannot be approved.

GroupSathi enforces strict financial discipline through automated, date-locked saving controls.

On the EMI Day
The "Pay EMI" button is active. Members can submit their monthly saving. Leaders can send a manual alert to all members.
Any Other Day
The "Pay EMI" button is disabled and locked. Any backend attempt to bypass the lock is rejected automatically.

Automated Reminder System:

  • A background service runs daily, detecting when EMI day is exactly 24 hours away.
  • It sends personalized notifications to every member — borrowers get loan-specific reminders, and all members get a general savings reminder.
  • A reminder_logs system ensures no member is ever sent duplicate reminders for the same monthly cycle.
Leaders and Co-Leaders can manually trigger an EMI alert on the EMI day using the "Send EMI Alert" button from the group details page.

GroupSathi uses a two-tier loan system to protect group capital based on loan risk level.

Normal Loans (≤ ₹50,000)
Approved directly by the Leader or Co-Leader. Requires mortgage details, loan amount, and repayment tenure. No consensus needed.
High-Value Loans (> ₹50,000)
Requires a verified Guarantor Profile (name, contact, address), a signed Agreement Document upload, and 100% unanimous approval from every active member. Even one rejection permanently rejects the loan.

Loan Extension Requests:

  • Borrowers can request extra repayment months from the group details page.
  • Extensions require consensus approval from all leaders and co-leaders before the tenure is extended.
Members can only have one active loan at a time. A second loan request is blocked until the current one is fully repaid.

GroupSathi uses a day-wise interest model. Outstanding loan values and accrued interest are updated in real-time based on how many days the loan has been active. Borrowers choose how to repay:

Option A: Pay Principal
Directly reduces your core outstanding loan balance. Since interest is calculated day-wise on the remaining principal, this instantly reduces all future interest charges too.
Option B: Pay Interest Only
Settles only the interest accumulated to the current date. The principal balance remains unchanged, letting borrowers clear pending charges without reducing the loan body.
Repayment requests are submitted by the borrower and require leader/co-leader approval before they are applied to the loan ledger. The group financial balance updates automatically upon approval.

GroupSathi has two types of fines with separate waiver workflows:

1. Late EMI Fine (Automatic):

  • Applied automatically when a member pays their EMI late.
  • The member can request a waiver from the group detail page → "Request Late Fine Waiver".
  • They record a verbal explanation using the in-app microphone and type a written reason.
  • 100% consensus from all other group members is required to waive it.

2. Consensus-Imposed Fine:

  • Leaders or Co-Leaders can initiate a fine imposition on any member for misconduct.
  • The fine is only imposed if all other active members collectively approve it.
  • The targeted member can request a waiver, which again requires unanimous approval from the group (excluding themselves).
The in-app voice recorder on the waiver request page allows members to record their verbal explanation directly in the browser — no external app needed.

When a group's cycle is complete or the group is being wound down, the Distribute Money feature allows a fair distribution of the group's financial pool to all members.

How it works:

  1. Go to your group page (or My Groups) and click "Distribute Money". Only Leaders and Co-Leaders can access this.
  2. A Settlement Preview page calculates the total group assets: EMI pool + interest collected + fines collected − outstanding loan balances.
  3. Each member's fair share is calculated proportionally based on their EMI contributions.
  4. After reviewing the breakdown, the Leader clicks Execute Settlement to finalize the distribution. This action clears all pending loans and settles any unpaid fines.
What Gets Cleared
All pending EMI requests, outstanding loan balances, and unpaid imposed fines are settled and marked complete.
Important Note
Settlement is a permanent action. Review the distribution preview carefully before executing. This is typically used at the end of a group's financial year or cycle.
Only the Group Leader can execute the final settlement. Co-Leaders can view the preview but cannot finalize it.

If you encounter any technical issues or need assistance, GroupSathi offers a professional ticketing system to ensure your queries are resolved promptly.

Ticketing Features:

Auto Assignment
When you raise a ticket, your Member ID and Mobile Number are auto-filled, and the ticket is instantly assigned to a random available Technical Staff member.
Escalation System
If a Technical Staff member needs higher clearance, they can escalate the ticket directly to Platform Admins for review. Both staff and admins receive real-time alerts.

Tracking & Reviews:

  • Dynamic Progress Map: You can track your ticket's live status (Raised → In Progress → [Admin Review] → Resolved) visually from your dashboard.
  • Resolution SLA: The platform maintains a strict 7-working-day limit to resolve customer issues.
  • 5-Star Reviews: Once your issue is resolved, you can rate your experience (1-5 stars) and leave a written review for the staff.
You can attach screenshots (up to 1MB, JPG/PNG) directly to your support chat to help our technical staff understand the issue better!
